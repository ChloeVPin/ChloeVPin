### also out in the world

two mac apps that live on their own sites:

- [**Deletely**](https://deletelymac.vercel.app) — maps the forward delete key to actually deleting files in finder. 13 MB, 12 MB RAM, 0% CPU.
- [**Caffeinate**](https://caffeinatemac.vercel.app) — menu bar app that drives `/usr/bin/caffeinate` with 1, 2 and 4 hour timers. no dependencies.
